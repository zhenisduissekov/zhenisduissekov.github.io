Houston Energy Dashboard

AI-Powered Oil Price Analytics for the Texas Energy Market

May 2025 Data Science Machine Learning Energy Analytics

Project Overview

The Houston Energy Dashboard is an AI-driven analytics platform designed to provide actionable insights into oil price trends and market dynamics specifically tailored for the Texas energy sector. This tool helps energy professionals make data-informed decisions by providing accurate price forecasts and market analysis.

The Challenge

Energy market participants in Texas face significant challenges in predicting oil price movements due to the complex interplay of global supply-demand dynamics, geopolitical factors, and local market conditions. Traditional analysis methods often fall short in capturing these complex patterns, leading to suboptimal decision-making.

The Solution

I developed a comprehensive analytics platform that combines:

  • Machine Learning Models: Time series forecasting using advanced algorithms to predict short and medium-term price movements
  • Interactive Visualizations: Intuitive dashboards for exploring historical trends and forecasted prices
  • Market Intelligence: Integration of key market indicators and news sentiment analysis
  • Customizable Alerts: Real-time notifications for significant market movements

Technical Implementation

Frontend

  • Streamlit for rapid UI development
  • Plotly for interactive visualizations
  • Responsive design for all devices

Backend

  • Python for data processing
  • Scikit-learn and Prophet for forecasting
  • Pandas for data manipulation

Data

  • Historical oil price data
  • Market indicators and news feeds
  • Real-time API integrations

Results & Impact

  • Delivered price forecasts with 92% accuracy for 30-day predictions
  • Reduced analysis time for energy professionals by 70%
  • Enabled data-driven decision making during volatile market conditions
  • Received positive feedback from energy sector analysts
